Privacy Policy for PetaPikir

Effective Date: November 16, 2026 · Package: com.aistudio.petapikir.krmxvp

PetaPikir ("the App") is a mind-mapping application for Android. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles your information when you use local storage and optional Google Drive synchronization.

1. Information We Access

  • Mind Map Content: Titles, node text, notes, colors, and tree structure that you create in the App.
  • Google Account & Drive Access (Optional): If you tap Hubungkan Drive (Connect Drive), PetaPikir requests OAuth access using the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive scope solely to locate, read, and sync your PetaPikir folder (including shared PetaPikir folders for collaboration between Google Drive users), and retrieves your Google account email address via Google Drive's about endpoint solely to display which account is connected.

2. How Your Data Is Used

  • Your mind maps are stored locally on your device using an on-device database so you can work offline.
  • When Google Drive sync is enabled, your mind map JSON files are stored in a single PetaPikir folder in Google Drive and synchronized two ways across your devices and collaborators who share that folder.
  • PetaPikir does not process unrelated files in your Google Drive, does not display ads, and does not sell, rent, or transfer your data to third parties or external developer servers.

3. Data Retention & Deletion

  • You can delete any mind map inside the App or directly from the PetaPikir folder in your Google Drive at any time.
  • You can disconnect your Google account at any time inside the App using Putuskan Akun, or revoke PetaPikir's permissions from your Google Account Security settings at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Uninstalling the App removes local on-device data; files already synced to your Google Drive remain in your Drive until you delete them.

4. Limited Use Disclosure

PetaPikir's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
